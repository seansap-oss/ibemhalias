End-to-End Revenue & ERP

Unified Online UPI + Offline Cash ERP & Auto-Enrollment

Online UPI Admissions (Emerald Green)

  • Student scans Institute QR Photo or Live Amount QR on /courses
  • Enters Name, Receipt Email, WhatsApp Phone & UPI Ref
  • Admin clicks Verify & Enroll → auto-creates student login, assigns course, emails receipt & prepares 1-click WhatsApp

Offline Cash / Desk Admissions (Royal Blue)

  • Walk-in Cash, Bank/NEFT, or Card/POS recorded at the counter
  • Instantly enrolls the student in the selected course
  • Combines with Online UPI in Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Yearly ERP reports

Expenses & GST Tracking (Rose)

  • Logs Salaries, Rent, Printing, Marketing & Server costs
  • Automatic GST / Tax calculation & Net Profit view

AI Revenue Forecast (Violet)

  • Computes Weekly & Monthly growth projections from live ledger trends
  • Interactive Donut Mix & Bar + Forecast charts
Governance & Staff Control

Role Hierarchy & Admin Accountability

Director

  • Creates & manages Admin 1..Admin 10
  • Views Per-Admin Daily Scorecard (logins/day, active minutes, live status)
  • Audits every Admin action & configures Idle Auto-Sign-Out (30s countdown)

Admin Staff

  • Builds Courses, uploads PDFs/Videos, manages Landing Studio
  • Verifies UPI orders & records walk-in Cash admissions
  • Generates AI MCQ & Essay exams

Teachers & Students

  • Teachers host Live Classes, moderate Raise-Hand & Q&A
  • Students read watermarked Kindle PDFs, watch protected videos & take AI exams
