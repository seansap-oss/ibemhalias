The Mauryan Empire was founded by Chandragupta Maurya in the late fourth century BCE.
He was assisted by Chanakya, who is traditionally associated with the Arthashastra.
Ashoka, the grandson of Chandragupta Maurya, later became one of the best-known Mauryan rulers.
After the Kalinga war, Ashoka promoted dhamma and issued inscriptions on rocks and pillars.
