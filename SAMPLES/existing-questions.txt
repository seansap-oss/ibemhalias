1. Who founded the Mauryan Empire?
A. Ashoka
B. Chandragupta Maurya
C. Bindusara
D. Kanishka
Answer: B
Explanation: Chandragupta Maurya founded the Mauryan Empire.

2. Which Article of the Constitution of India abolishes untouchability?
A. Article 14
B. Article 15
C. Article 17
D. Article 19
Answer: C
Explanation: Article 17 abolishes untouchability.

3. The Reserve Bank of India commenced operations in which year?
A. 1930
B. 1935
C. 1947
D. 1949
Answer: B
Explanation: RBI commenced operations on 1 April 1935.
