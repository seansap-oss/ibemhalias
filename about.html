Our Story

The journey of IBEMH ALIAS from a vision to becoming India's most innovative UPSC coaching platform.

Our Journey

Transforming UPSC Preparation Since 2015

IBEMH ALIAS was born from a simple yet powerful idea — that every UPSC aspirant deserves personalized guidance tailored to their unique strengths, weaknesses, and learning style. Founded by a group of IAS officers and education technology experts, we set out to bridge the gap between traditional coaching methods and modern AI-powered learning.

Over the years, we've continuously evolved, integrating cutting-edge AI technology with proven pedagogical methods. Our platform has grown from serving local aspirants to becoming a pan-India community of over 15,000+ students, helping them achieve their dreams of serving the nation.

Today, IBEMH ALIAS stands as a pioneer in AI-powered UPSC coaching, offering personalized study plans, intelligent answer evaluation, automated current affairs summarization, and mock interviews that simulate real UPSC panels. We're not just a coaching institute — we're your personal guide to cracking UPSC.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower every UPSC aspirant with personalized, AI-driven education that maximizes their potential to serve the nation through public service.

  • Make quality education accessible to every corner of India
  • Blend traditional mentorship with advanced technology
  • Focus on conceptual understanding over rote learning
  • Provide continuous assessment and personalized feedback

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted and innovative UPSC coaching platform, where technology meets tradition to create the next generation of civil servants.

  • Lead the future of UPSC education with AI innovation
  • Create a community of 100,000+ successful civil servants
  • Be the benchmark for result-oriented coaching
  • Expand globally, serving Indian diaspora aspirants worldwide
