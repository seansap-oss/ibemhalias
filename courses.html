Yes, you can switch between online and offline modes anytime during your course duration. Your course materials and progress will be synced across both platforms.

Classes are conducted live Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Recordings are available 24x7 for revision.

Yes, we provide comprehensive study material including GS notes, optional subject guides, current affairs monthly compilations, and digital resources through our LMS.
